Privacy Policy

Vinyl Curator · last updated August 2026

Vinyl Curator publishes a personal vinyl record archive at vinylcurator.net and hosts private, access-controlled record archives for individual collectors. This policy explains what information is handled and how.

The public archive

The public pages at vinylcurator.net are static and do not require an account. We do not use analytics or tracking cookies on them, and we do not collect personal information from visitors who browse them.

Private client archives

A private archive is shown only to the specific person it belongs to. To confirm who you are, sign-in is handled by Cloudflare Access using your Google account. In that process we receive your email address, which we use for one purpose only: to check that you are authorised to view your own archive. We do not use it for marketing and do not share it.

A session cookie set by Cloudflare Access keeps you signed in for the duration of your session. The record photographs and pressing details in a private archive are provided by, and shown only to, that archive's owner.

Photographs you share

Where you engage the documentation or hosting service, the photographs and details you provide are used to build and host your archive. They are stored in private storage and served only to you. You may ask for your archive and its photographs to be removed at any time; on request they are deleted.

What we don't do

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers that operate the service (Cloudflare for access control and storage, Google for sign-in), acting on our behalf.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@vinylcurator.net.
