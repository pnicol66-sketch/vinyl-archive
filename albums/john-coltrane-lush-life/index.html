The Album

Lush Life gathers two distinct dates from Coltrane's early Prestige years: a 1957 trio session with Earl May and Art Taylor, and a 1958 quintet date with Donald Byrd and members of Miles Davis's rhythm section, Red Garland and Paul Chambers, plus drummers Louis Hayes and Albert Heath. Coltrane was still a working sideman-turned-emerging-leader at this point, only a few years removed from his tenure with Davis and Thelonious Monk, and the album captures him refining the harmonic vocabulary that would soon flower into his classic quartet period. The Label

The side labels show the classic Prestige yellow "fireworks" design - black rim with yellow lightning-bolt/star graphics, "PRESTIGE" arched in yellow-on-black at top, "LONG PLAYING MICROGROOVE" arched at bottom, a spiral "@" logo and starburst flanking the top arc, and the 203 South Washington Ave., Bergenfield, N.J. address printed above the title - this is the address/design Prestige used from 1958 into the early-1960s before the West 50th St. New York address replaced it. This is a deep-groove pressing on both sides. This Bergenfield-address, RVG-stamped, deep-groove yellow label is the first-pressing design for PRLP 7188, ahead of the mid-1960s reissue with the same catalogue number but a later, non-deep-groove label.

Fidelity

Rudy Van Gelder recorded and cut the lacquers at his Hackensack studio for this original Prestige pressing, and the RVG-stamped, deep-groove first pressing is the cut collectors on the forums generally regard as the most vivid and immediate-sounding version of this material, ahead of the mid-1960s Prestige reissue and the 1968 electronically reprocessed "stereo" PRST 7581 edition. The 2003 Analogue Productions 45 RPM mono reissue (mastered from the original tapes) is the modern audiophile benchmark and is often preferred for surface noise and dynamics, but the original RVG mono pressing remains the historical reference for tonal character. Pressing quality on this Abbey Record Mfg. copy is typical of early-1960s Prestige manufacturing - generally solid, if occasionally variable in vinyl formulation compared to later, quieter reissue pressings.

Notes

1961 Prestige PRLP 7188 mono - original first pressing. Yellow fireworks label with the 203 South Washington Ave., Bergenfield, N.J. address, deep groove on both sides, and RVG stamps with AB plant etch in the runout, matching the first-issue configuration. The runouts read "PRLP-7188 A RVG AB" / "PRLP-7188-B RVG AB A", consistent with this as the first-pressing edition rather than the 1964 reissue with the same catalogue number.
