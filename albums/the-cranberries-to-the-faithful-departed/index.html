The Album

To the Faithful Departed (1996) is the Cranberries' third album, recorded in a burst of activity in November-December 1995 at Windmill Lane in Dublin and Mushroom Studios in Vancouver with producer Bruce Fairbairn, whose stadium-rock pedigree with Aerosmith and AC/DC pushed the band toward a harder, heavier sound than the dreamy jangle of their first two records. It is an album steeped in grief, dedicated to two men who died in 1995: Dolores O'Riordan's grandfather Joe, elegized in 'Joe', and Denny Cordell, the Shelter Records co-founder who had championed the Limerick band from their earliest days and is mourned in 'Cordell'. Elsewhere O'Riordan takes on heroin ('Salvation'), press intrusion ('Free to Decide'), the Bosnian war ('Bosnia'), and gun violence ('I Just Shot John Lennon'), while the love song 'When You're Gone' acquired a haunting second life after O'Riordan's own death in January 2018. Arriving on the heels of 'Zombie' and the multi-platinum No Need to Argue, the album was a commercial juggernaut - number 1 in six countries including France, Belgium and Sweden, number 2 in the UK, and the band's highest-ever US album chart placing - selling over six million copies worldwide. Critics were far cooler than the public, finding Fairbairn's arena-rock production strident beside the band's earlier delicacy, though Entertainment Weekly gave it an A- and called it their best album. It remains their most debated record, its raw mourning and O'Riordan's keening, one-of-a-kind voice aging well under reassessment, and it closed out the band's imperial commercial phase as the biggest Irish alternative act of the 1990s after U2.

The Label

Modern grey Island label with the circular palm logo, LC 00407, BIEM/SDRM rights box, dual '(c) 1996 / (c) 2023 UMG Recordings, Inc.' lines, and rim text citing Universal International Music B.V., Hilversum, Netherlands with 'Made in the EU' - the standard UMe/Universal catalog-reissue label template of the 2020s, not any period Island design. Island has been a UMG (formerly PolyGram) property since the 1989 PolyGram acquisition, and its 1990s catalog is now managed by UMe, which is why the 2023 copyright sits alongside the 1996 original. The 'Universal Music Recordings' signature box and the 557####/006024557##### numbering are current Universal EU catalog conventions. This places the copy unambiguously in the 13 October 2023 deluxe reissue campaign, two design generations removed from the 1996 original single LP (Island 524 314-1).

Fidelity

All tracks remastered from the original audio by John Dent at Metropolis (official UMe credit - note the collector's jacket transcription reading 'John Davis' is a misread; Universal's press materials uniformly credit John Dent, consistent with the 'JD' deadwax etch on side A), pressed in Germany - typically flat, quiet, well-centered EU vinyl. As with virtually all UMe catalog remasters it is cut from high-resolution digital transfers rather than all-analog tape, yielding a louder, denser modern master than the 1996 cut. A genuine point in its favor: the roughly 55-minute program is spread across three sides here instead of being crammed onto a single 1996 LP, easing inner-groove distortion. No audiophile edition (MoFi, Analogue Productions, Speakers Corner) exists for this title, so the fidelity hierarchy is essentially the scarce 1996 Island original single LP (favored for period dynamics) versus this 2023 deluxe (favored for quiet surfaces, generous groove spacing, and the exclusive Paris demos).

Notes

2023 Island/UMe European deluxe 2LP reissue/remaster (released 13 October 2023), not a 1996 original pressing. Decisive evidence: grey Island labels carrying dual '(c) 1996 / (c) 2023 UMG Recordings' lines with UMG Hilversum rim text; the hype sticker advertising three previously unreleased demos recorded in Paris in 1995; and the four-side configuration with bonus material on side D. The 'Made in Germany' sticker and the typed deadwax job numbers 62082/62083 confirm an EU pressing, and the 'JD' etch on side A marks remastering engineer John Dent of Metropolis. The true original is the 1996 Island single LP 524 314-1 with period labels and no bonus side.
