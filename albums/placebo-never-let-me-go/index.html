This Pressing

2022 worldwide standard pressing (stereo) - So Recordings SOAKLP263 black vinyl gatefold 2xLP, pressed by Optimal Media; a later/standard issue, not one of the title's limited colour-vinyl variants.

The Album

Never Let Me Go arrived in 2022 as Placebo's first studio album in nearly a decade, made by Brian Molko and Stefan Olsdal operating for the first time as a stripped-down duo after the departure of longtime drummer Steve Forrest. The songs were written and recorded against a backdrop that included the pandemic and the war in Ukraine, giving the record's lyrics a political and environmental edge unusual even for a band long associated with dark, confessional subject matter. Molko and Olsdal brought in vintage synthesizers alongside guitars and session drummers, broadening the group's sound beyond the gothic alt-rock of their 1990s and 2000s work. Placebo had already built a long career defined by androgynous glam-rock theatrics, gender-fluid imagery and hits that made them one of the defining alternative bands to emerge from the Britpop era's margins. Critical reaction to Never Let Me Go was generally positive, with reviewers noting a mature, socially engaged Placebo while debating whether the record matched the band's commercial peak. It extended the catalogue of a band still capable of attracting a dedicated fanbase and chart placement more than twenty-five years into its existence.

The Label

Both side labels carry a hot-pink/magenta ground with white print, the stylised “SO RECORDINGS” logo centred at the bottom, and the ℗ & © 2022 Elevator Lady Ltd. line under exclusive licence to So Recordings - a design first used for this title's 2022 release and not seen before that year. The printed catalogue number varies between sides (SOAKLP283 on sides 1, 2 and 4, SOAKLP263 on side 3), an inconsistency within this copy's own labels rather than a sign of a different pressing, since all four runouts carry the same SOAKLP263 code. No deep groove is present on any of the flat, modern-pressing labels. The 2022 copyright date on the label is the earliest this copy can have been made; it cannot predate 2022 and places this pressing at the start of the title's vinyl chronology rather than after it.

Fidelity

The album was mastered by Robin Schmidt at 24-96 Mastering and mixed by Adam Noble, a contemporary digital-era chain typical of modern major/indie rock releases rather than an audiophile all-analogue cut. Pressing is by Optimal Media, a high-volume European plant generally regarded by collectors as solid but unremarkable, with quality variance reported across different titles pressed there. Collectors discussing the record treat this standard black-vinyl pressing as sonically equivalent to the title's limited colour-vinyl runs, since all derive from the same mastering and plant; there is no audiophile reissue series (no Analogue Productions, Mobile Fidelity or equivalent) that outranks it. As the baseline worldwide vinyl edition, it sits level with the title's other 2022 colour-vinyl variants in fidelity terms, differentiated from them only by vinyl colour and scarcity, not by mastering.

Notes

This 2022 worldwide standard-issue black vinyl pressing of Never Let Me Go carries the catalogue number SOAKLP263 in a gatefold jacket with a lyric/credits insert. Its labels print 283 on three sides and 263 on the fourth, while all four runouts carry the matching SOAKLP263 code, a single catalogue identity running through the pressing. The runout on every side names 24-96 Mastering and an Optimal Media job/plant code, and the ℗/© line on the labels reads 2022, the earliest year this pressing can carry. This is the plain black-vinyl standard edition, distinct from the title's limited colour-vinyl, picture-disc and clear-vinyl pressings.
