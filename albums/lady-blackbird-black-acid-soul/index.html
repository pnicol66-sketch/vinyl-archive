The Album

Black Acid Soul is the 2021 debut album by Lady Blackbird, the reborn persona of Los Angeles singer Marley Munroe, who had spent years as a journeyman vocalist under other names before producer Chris Seefried helped her recast herself in the image of the great jazz-soul divas. Recorded largely live in Sunset Sound's Studio B in Hollywood - a room steeped in Prince and Doors history - the album strips soul, jazz and blues to voice, piano, double bass and brushed percussion, putting Munroe's huge, scarred, Nina Simone-evoking voice front and center. The set is built on daring reinterpretations: Simone's civil-rights anthem "Blackbird" opens the record as a statement of identity, Irma Thomas's "Ruler Of My Heart" (itself a reworking of "Time Is On My Side") becomes a slow-burn showcase, and Tim Hardin's "It'll Never Happen Again" closes the cycle with devastating restraint, alongside originals like the title track co-written with Seefried. Trombone Shorty drops in a trumpet cameo on the gospel-inflected "Nobody's Sweetheart," underscoring the album's New Orleans soul undercurrents. Critics on both sides of the Atlantic hailed it as one of the standout vocal debuts of the year, with particular praise for Munroe's ability to sound simultaneously ancient and new, and it established Lady Blackbird as a major festival and jazz-club draw. The record taps directly into the lineage of Simone, Billie Holiday and Esther Phillips while remaining unmistakably contemporary in its stark, minimalist production.

The Label

The side labels are matte black with the giant stacked white "BLACK ACID SOUL" wordmark (Side A) and "LADY BLACKBIRD" wordmark (Side B), Foundation and BMG logos at the bottom, and rim text reading "Manufactured in Germany. GEMA/BIEM" with the numbers LC19813, 538711481 and 4050538711486 printed around the perimeter; no deep groove, consistent with a modern European pressing. This is the standard trade black-vinyl issue: the label carries the printed catalogue number in the runout area rather than the FMP 008 etched matrix found on the 500-copy Bandcamp/Rough Trade colorway exclusives. Foundation Music Productions licensed the album to BMG Rights Management (UK) in 2021, and this German-manufactured pressing was the wide retail configuration sold throughout the UK/EU from the album's September 2021 release. It therefore sits in the first-issue window, simultaneous with - not before or after - the limited colored variants.

Fidelity

Mastered by Bernie Grundman at Bernie Grundman Mastering, Hollywood, from Seth Atkins Horan's mixes - a high-end credit for a modern indie release, and the spare voice-and-piano production plays to Grundman's strengths in warmth and midrange clarity. All 2021 vinyl variants, black and colored alike, derive from this same Grundman master, so sonics are equivalent across the family. The caveat is pressing consistency: user reports across nearly every variant of this title cite surface noise, pops and occasional warps from the European plant, making individual copy quality the variable rather than the cut. The 2022 2xLP Deluxe adds remixes but uses the same core mastering, and no audiophile-tier reissue (Analogue Productions, MoFi, etc.) exists as of this writing - the Grundman-mastered LP is the top vinyl tier available.
