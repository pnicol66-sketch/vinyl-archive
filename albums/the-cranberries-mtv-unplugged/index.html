The Album

The Cranberries taped their MTV Unplugged set on February 14, 1995 at the Howard Gilman Opera House in Brooklyn, at the commercial height of their breakthrough second album No Need to Argue, which had turned the Limerick quartet fronted by Dolores O'Riordan into one of the biggest alternative rock acts in the world. Stripped of the layered electric guitars that defined the studio records, the acoustic format - the core trio of Noel Hogan, Mike Hogan and Fergal Lawler joined by supplemental strings - threw O'Riordan's keening, ornamented vocal delivery into sharp relief, giving "Linger," "Ode to My Family" and a hushed, harrowing "Zombie" a fragile intimacy the studio versions didn't have. The broadcast aired on MTV in April 1995 and quickly became beloved among fans, but the audio was never given an official release, leaving it to circulate for decades as a heavily traded and treasured bootleg. It captures the band at its commercial and artistic peak, just before the pressures of global fame and O'Riordan's own struggles began to complicate the group's trajectory, and it stands as a vivid document of how completely her voice could carry a song without a wall of sound behind it. Critics and longtime fans have long regarded this taping as among the strongest entries in the entire MTV Unplugged series, prized for its emotional directness and the acoustic reinvention of songs that were already era-defining on record. Its eventual full release became one of the emotional touchstones of the archival campaign the surviving members undertook following O'Riordan's death in January 2018, cementing the performance's status as an essential, career-spanning companion piece to the band's studio catalog.

The Label

This copy carries the 2025 Island Records/UMe picture labels (MTV Unplugged logo, Island palm-tree logo, rim text crediting "An Island Records release... under exclusive license to UMG Recordings, Inc., \u00a92025 UMG Recordings, Inc."), the standard US configuration rather than the Europe/Czech variant, which carries its own "Made in Czech Republic" sticker and separate mastering credit. The printed catalog/barcode is 602478707520, while the deadwax embeds an internal Universal reference (00602475914693 E/F) that Universal reuses across formats of this title; the printed catalog is the correct identifier for cataloging purposes. Discogs documents this exact pressing (matrix 00602475914693E/F, barcode 602478707520, gatefold jacket) as release r35599996, "Made in Mexico," distinguishing it from the Europe/Czech pressing and the Complex-exclusive alabaster-white edition (r35618833). As the first-ever standalone vinyl release of this 1995 performance, this Mexico-pressed black LP represents the baseline first standalone pressing in the release hierarchy.

Fidelity

Mastered from the archived 1995 MTV broadcast tapes for this 2025 release, the first officially sanctioned audio treatment of a recording previously known only through bootlegs; the standalone US Unplugged mastering credit is not clearly documented on this variant, unlike the Europe/Czech pressing which credits Mystery Room Mastering & Recording Co. Early collector reports on Discogs are mixed-to-positive, generally describing the pressing as full-bodied and clean with O'Riordan's vocal well separated from the strings and acoustic guitars, though some buyers note surface noise or off-center pressing issues on individual copies. As an in-print, mass-manufactured modern reissue there is no meaningful vintage fidelity hierarchy to rank against - the alabaster-white Complex-exclusive edition and the Europe/Czech pressing are contemporaries pressed from the same digital transfer rather than superior audiophile alternatives, with buyer reports suggesting the alabaster-white edition may have a slight edge in perceived clarity.

Notes

2025 Island/UMe 602478707520 stereo LP is the first-ever standalone pressing of this 1995 performance, released November 2025. Matches Discogs release r35599996, documented as "Made in Mexico," gatefold jacket, matrix 00602475914693E/F, distinguishing it from the separately manufactured Europe/Czech pressing (Mystery Room mastering, Czech Republic sticker) and the Complex-exclusive alabaster-white alternate-cover pressing (barcode 602478853487). The only earlier vinyl appearance of this performance was inside the 2024 No Need to Argue 30th-anniversary box set. Hype sticker text ("First Time Released In Its Entirety") and standard black vinyl confirm this is the regular retail edition rather than the colored-vinyl exclusive.
