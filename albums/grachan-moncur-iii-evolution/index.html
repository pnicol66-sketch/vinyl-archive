This Pressing

2022 Blue Note Classic Vinyl Series reissue (stereo) - Kevin Gray lacquer cut at Cohearent Audio, pressed by Optimal Media in Germany; a New York USA-style label carrying an LC 00133 code and a 2022 copyright line places it as a modern pressing, not an original-era copy.

The Album

Trombonist Grachan Moncur III had already made his mark as a sideman and composer for Jackie McLean before stepping out as a leader on this 1963 session, his second date for the label following Some Other Stuff. Moncur wrote all four pieces here, building loosely structured, harmonically open compositions that let the front line of Lee Morgan, Jackie McLean and Moncur himself stretch beyond conventional hard-bop changes while Bobby Hutcherson's vibraphone added a shimmering, almost orchestral color to the ensemble. Bob Cranshaw's bass and the young Tony Williams's drumming, fresh from his work with Miles Davis, gave the date a flexible, conversational rhythmic foundation suited to the music's freer harmonic language. The record arrived at a moment when a cluster of Blue Note artists - McLean, Moncur, Hutcherson, Williams among them - were pushing the label's hard-bop foundation toward the more abstract vocabulary of the era's new jazz without abandoning its melodic and blues roots. Evolution has long been regarded as one of the quieter but most influential statements of that mid-1960s transitional period, admired for the compositional ambition of pieces like the title track and 'The Coaster' and for the chemistry of its young front line. It cemented Moncur's reputation as a composer of real sophistication and remains a touchstone for the album's blend of structure and openness within the Blue Note idiom.

The Label

The side labels show the cream-and-blue New York USA design - BLUE up the left, NOTE along the foot, the rim reading BLUE NOTE RECORDS INC • NEW YORK USA with no street address and a registered mark under NOTE - the same layout Blue Note used from the early 1960s into 1967. On this copy that design sits alongside an LC 00133 label code, a 2022 Capitol Records copyright line, and a Made in the EU credit, none of which could exist on a label printed before 1976, let alone 1964; a label code and a 2022 copyright line date the printing, not the design it reproduces. The jacket also carries a second catalogue style, ST-84153, alongside the labels' BST 84153, a small discrepancy of the kind common to reissue packaging. Taken together these marks place this copy as a modern pressing that faithfully reproduces the original New York USA label art rather than an example of it.

Fidelity

The lacquers were cut by Kevin Gray at Cohearent Audio from the original analog tapes, with vinyl pressed at Optimal Media in Germany as part of Blue Note's 180-gram Classic Vinyl Series. Collectors generally rate Kevin Gray's Blue Note Classic Series cuts as clean, accurate modern analog transfers, a dependable and musical alternative to scarce originals. Within this title's fidelity hierarchy it sits below the original Van Gelder-cut New York USA first pressing and below the audiophile Music Matters 45rpm two-LP edition, but above standard-issue reissues and the CD.

Notes

This copy is a 2022 worldwide reissue in Blue Note's Classic Vinyl Series, not an original 1964 pressing. The label reproduces the New York USA cream-and-blue design but carries an LC 00133 label code and a ℗© 2022 Capitol Records line, both marks that cannot predate 1976 and 2022 respectively. The runout is etched rather than hand-stamped and carries a Kevin Gray/Cohearent Audio mastering code alongside an Optimal Media (Germany) plant code dated to 2022, confirming the pressing source and date. The jacket keeps its original shrink.
