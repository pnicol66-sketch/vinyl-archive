This Pressing

1975 Blue Note Re-Issue Series pressing (BN-LA401-H2) - United Artists-era blue label, Columbia Terre Haute stamper, gatefold 2xLP compilation.

The Album

This compilation gathers Sonny Rollins’s 1956-57 Blue Note dates, the stretch that cemented his reputation as the decade’s most commanding tenor voice in hard bop. It draws on sessions with Donald Byrd, Wynton Kelly, Max Roach and Gene Ramey from December 1956, a spring 1957 quintet with J.J. Johnson, Thelonious Monk, Paul Chambers and Art Blakey, a September 1957 trio with Kelly, Doug Watkins and Philly Joe Jones, and a single trio performance taped live at the Village Vanguard with Wilbur Ware and Elvin Jones that November. The Vanguard cut anticipates the trio format Rollins would make famous on his own live Vanguard album from the same month, and the studio sides show him refining the thematic improvising and long unaccompanied lines that became his signature. Pairing him with Monk on “Misterioso” and “Reflections” places two of the era’s most original composer-improvisers in direct conversation. Rollins’s Blue Note period sits between his Prestige work and his classic 1956-57 run of leader dates, a stretch widely regarded as the high point of his early career and a cornerstone of modern tenor saxophone playing. The material collected here has been prized by critics and musicians alike for the fluency, wit and harmonic daring Rollins brought to both originals and standards.

The Label

All four labels carry the all-blue “THE BLUE NOTE RE-ISSUE SERIES” design: a large white “b” carrying a blue note at the upper left (centred on Side 3), the series name arched around it, and rim text naming United Artists Music and Records Group, Inc., Los Angeles, California 90028, Made in U.S.A. The labels are flat, without a deep groove. This design and rim credit belong to United Artists’ ownership of Blue Note (1971-79) and were used for the mid-1970s “Re-Issue Series” compilations, of which BN-LA401-H2 is one; the ℗1975 date printed on the label is the earliest this pressing could have been made. This pressing carries the Columbia Terre Haute-pressed stampers of this issue, a later pressing standing apart from the parallel All Disc Records or alternate-font pressings carrying the same catalogue number, and well below the 1950s original Blue Note mono pressings of the source material.

Fidelity

The source material was recorded by Rudy Van Gelder at his Hackensack studio and on location at the Village Vanguard, but this 1975 compilation was remastered and manufactured under United Artists’ Blue Note ownership at a contract pressing plant rather than cut specifically for audiophile playback. Collectors generally regard the Re-Issue Series as a convenient, inexpensive way to own this material rather than a sonic showcase, and rank it below the original mono Blue Note LPs of the individual source albums and below later audiophile reissues of those same albums. As a various-sessions compilation there is no audiophile-series counterpart to this specific pairing of tracks.

Notes

This is the 1975 “Blue Note Re-Issue Series” double-LP gatefold compilation of Sonny Rollins’s 1956-57 Blue Note recordings, not an original-era pressing. The label is the all-blue United Artists-era design crediting United Artists Music and Records Group, Inc., Los Angeles, with the ℗1975 date printed on every side - a date the copy cannot predate. The runout on all four sides reads T1 BN-LA401-1-1 through -4-1, matching the catalogue number exactly with no deeper plant or stamper variation visible. Pressed under United Artists’ ownership of Blue Note, this configuration ranks well below the individual 1950s mono first pressings of the source albums in collector esteem, functioning instead as a compilation reissue.
