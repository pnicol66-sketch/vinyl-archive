The Album

Help Us Stranger marked the long-awaited return of The Raconteurs, the band Jack White formed with fellow Nashville singer-songwriter Brendan Benson along with the rhythm section of Jack Lawrence and Patrick Keeler, following an eleven-year gap since their second album. Recorded largely at White's own Third Man Studio in Nashville, the sessions found the quartet returning to the dual-vocalist, riff-driven garage and blues-rock sound that had defined their earlier work, while folding in moodier, more reflective material. The album includes a cover of Donovan's '60s folk tune, recast in the band's heavier register, alongside a set of new originals that move between stomping rockers and slower, melancholy ballads. Arriving amid renewed interest in White's many simultaneous projects, the record was received as a confident, well-crafted reunion that reaffirmed the chemistry between its two frontmen without chasing nostalgia. It extended the legacy of a band whose 2006 debut had already helped define 2000s garage-rock revivalism, and it was welcomed by critics as a mature, purposeful follow-up rather than a victory-lap reunion record.

The Label

Both sides carry a custom picture label made only for this title rather than a standard Third Man house design: Side 1 shows a single grid-patterned walking figure on a charcoal ground with the title in green and the Third Man globe logo set vertically below the spindle hole; Side 2 shows two of these pedestrian figures reaching toward one another on a dark grey/black ground, with the same vertical globe logo below the hole. Both labels print TMR 600, STEREO, 33 1/3 RPM and the ℗ & © 2019 Third Man Records, LLC line. Third Man Records only began operating its own Third Man Mastering and Third Man Pressing plant in Detroit from 2017-2019 onward, and the 2019 copyright line on the label is itself the earliest this copy could have been made - it cannot predate 2019. The design and printed data place this copy among the first Nashville/Detroit-era Third Man pressings of the title rather than a later repress sharing the same catalogue number.

Fidelity

The lacquers were cut at Third Man's own in-house mastering facility and pressed at the label's Detroit plant, both etched into the runout alongside the catalogue codes. Collectors on the forums generally regard Third Man's Detroit pressings of this era as solid, clean-sounding rock pressings without major quality complaints, though not singled out as audiophile-tier cuts. There is no audiophile remaster or specialist reissue series of this title to rank against; this standard stereo LP represents the album's standard commercial fidelity tier.

Notes

This 2019 Third Man Records TMR 600 stereo LP was pressed for the USA & Europe market at the label's own Detroit plant. Both sides carry custom picture labels made specifically for this title, printed with the ℗ & © 2019 Third Man Records, LLC line. The runouts on both sides carry the ACRES TO RUN / CORNEA RUST etchings together with WTS, TMM (Third Man Mastering) and TMP (Third Man Pressing) marks. No element of the record points to any issue earlier than 2019.
