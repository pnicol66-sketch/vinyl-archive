The Album

Recorded at Rudy Van Gelder's Englewood Cliffs studio across sessions in April and June 1962, Coltrane arrived as the saxophonist's regular working quartet with McCoy Tyner, Jimmy Garrison and Elvin Jones was solidifying into one of jazz's defining bands, with Roy Haynes sitting in on drums for the epic reworking of "Out of This World." The album balances Coltrane originals like the modal showcase "Miles' Mode" and the African-inflected "Tunji" against standards and show tunes - "Soul Eyes," "The Inch Worm" and the Harold Arlen/Johnny Mercer title that opens the record - stretched into extended, sheets-of-sound explorations. It sits at a pivotal point in Coltrane's Impulse! years, bridging the harmonic density of his Atlantic period with the freer, more modal and spiritually searching music that would culminate in A Love Supreme two years later. The record is widely regarded by critics and historians as an important transitional statement, showing Coltrane's quartet mastering long-form modal improvisation while still rooted in the American songbook. It remains a touchstone of his catalog and a favorite among listeners tracing the evolution of his sound through the early-to-mid 1960s.

The Label

The label photos show the orange centre with a grey/black perimeter band carrying the "impulse!" wordmark four times (alternating upright/inverted) around four white "step" logo blocks, catalogue numbers printed as "A-21-A"/"A-21-B" with "Side 1"/"Side 2", and the small print reading "A PRODUCT OF ABC-PARAMOUNT RECORDS, INC. / PRINTED IN U.S.A." - the back cover carries the identical ABC-Paramount wording. Per bsnpubs/londonjazzcollector, Impulse mono labels read "A PRODUCT OF AM-PAR RECORD CORP." 1960-63, switched to "A PRODUCT OF ABC-PARAMOUNT RECORDS, INC." for 1963-66/67, and only became "ABC RECORDS INC, New York, N.Y. 10019" for 1967-68; the "-A"/"-B" side suffix was added to US Impulse catalogue numbers starting in 1965. This copy's ABC-Paramount wording plus -A/-B suffix therefore places it as a mid-1960s (c.1965-66) mono repress, later than the original 1962 Am-Par first pressing (Discogs 875495, which prints "Am-Par Record Corp." with no -A/-B suffix) and earlier than the 1967 ABC Records relabelling that the catalogue server's matched Discogs release (15248748) actually describes - that block's own note states its labels read "A Product of ABC Records Inc.", which contradicts the ABC-Paramount wording seen in these photos, so 15248748 is not this exact copy despite sharing the A-21-A/-B catalogue format. Deep groove: cannot tell from the photos.

Fidelity

Van Gelder's original tape and lacquer work underlies this pressing, and the LW-stamped, Van Gelder-etched runout matches the marks documented on the earliest Am-Par lacquers, indicating no remastering occurred for this later label run. Collectors on forums.stevehoffman.tv generally rate any Van Gelder-stamped mono Impulse pressing of this title well above the later ABC/MCA reissues and well above the 1997 Ron McMaster remaster. Within the album's own fidelity hierarchy this ABC-Paramount-label repress sits just below a true first-pressing Am-Par copy (same stampers, later label print only) and above the 1967 ABC Records repress, the 1970s ABC Impulse reissues, and the digitally sourced later reissues; it also predates and generally outranks the mono-to-stereo era's rechanneled pressings.

Notes

The label and back-cover text read "A PRODUCT OF ABC-PARAMOUNT RECORDS, INC." with the -A/-B suffix on the catalogue number, placing this copy in the 1965-66 mono repress window per bsnpubs/londonjazzcollector label chronology - later than the 1962 Am-Par first pressing and earlier than the 1967 ABC Records relabel. The typed runout (A21-A/A21-B LW, Van Gelder stamped) matches the etched "LW" and stamped Van Gelder marks documented for the original lacquers (Discogs 875495), indicating the same stampers/mother continued to be used for this later label run. The catalogue server's matched Discogs release (15248748, 1967) describes ABC Records wording rather than ABC-Paramount, so it does not match this copy's visible label text and is not the correct variant page for it.
