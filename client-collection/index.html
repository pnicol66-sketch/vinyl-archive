A collection documented for you

Your collection, documented record by record — and kept entirely yours.

Every record is photographed, transcribed and identified to a fixed standard, then turned into whatever you need it to be:

  • —An individual record page and archive for every album, hostable privately on the web, exactly as shown here.
  • —A working sheet that prints individual album summaries, full collection schedules, and a collection schedule with researched values for insurance.
  • —Coffee-table catalogue books you design yourself, straight from the sheet menu — choose the layout and records, and export a print-ready book of your collection.
  • —Private by default — no prices are published, and the files stay in your own Google Drive.

Private, and yours

Your collection is never published publicly, it's hosted as your private collection behind your login. The photographs and files live in your own Google Drive, shared read-only for as long as you choose, and nothing about what you own is put on this site. The documentation is made for you — to insure, to sell, or simply to keep.
