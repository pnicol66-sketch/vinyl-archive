Private, and yours

Your collection is never published publicly — it's hosted as your private collection behind your login. If you choose to sell, you can open just the records you're offering, a page for buyers to browse, while the rest of the collection stays private. The photographs and files live in your own Google Drive, shared read-only for as long as you choose, and nothing about what you own is put on this site. The documentation is made for you — to insure, to sell, or simply to keep.
