Why I built this

I built this because I couldn’t prove what I owned. Thirty years and three countries of buying records, and no way to show anyone — an insurer, a valuer, a buyer — what was actually in the collection. What started as my own catalogue became a set of tools for anyone who needs to catalogue, document, insure or sell vinyl properly. Read the full story →
