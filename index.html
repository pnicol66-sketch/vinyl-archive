How a record gets documented

Everything that has to happen with the record in your hands happens in the app, in one pass. Everything after that happens without you.

  1. 01

    Capture

    You · in the app

    A fixed 13-entry checklist: front and back cover, every label, every disc face — each one auto-cropped for you. In the same pass you type the condition grades for the cover and each side, and type or dictate the matrix / runout straight off the disc.

  2. 02

    Upload

    You · one tap

    The set uploads to a folder in your own Google Drive, named from the artist and album, and you share that one folder read-only. Nothing else in your Drive is touched.

  3. 03

    Identify

    The archive · remotely

    Your matrix transcription and label shots go through deep research — pressing-plant and label archives, dating references, decades of auction and dealer sale records — read against thirty years of handling these records first-hand.

  4. 04

    Research & deliver

    The archive · delivered

    Your grades are set beside recorded sales of that exact variant, so the market history sits with the record. Out comes a documentation pack for a broker or buyer, a private record page, or bulk listings for eBay and Discogs.
